# Singapore - Ready-to-edit destination draft

## Destination name
Singapore

## Positioning
A city of vibrant contrasts, from Marina Bay icons and neighbourhood stories to green spaces, wildlife and island experiences.

## Destination description

Singapore brings together different worlds in one easy-to-explore city. Begin with the waterfront energy of Marina Bay, striking architecture and the gardens that have become symbols of a city always reinventing itself.

Walk through Little India, Chinatown or Kampong Gelam to discover streets shaped by food, culture, colour and everyday life. Spend time at the Singapore River, discover local favourites or simply slow down over a meal.

For families and curious travellers, Sentosa, the Mandai wildlife parks and Jewel Changi add another side to the journey. We help combine these possibilities into the right pace for your people and preferences.

## Featured Singapore journey

**Singapore, at Your Pace** - a suggested **4 days / 3 nights**.

- Day 1: Arrival and Marina Bay. Settle in, then discover the waterfront at an easy pace.
- Day 2: Gardens and Neighbourhoods. Discover Gardens by the Bay, local streets, culture and food.
- Day 3: Your Day, Your Way. Choose Sentosa, a Mandai wildlife park or a personal-interest day.
- Day 4: Easy Departure. A gentle morning and departure, depending on flight timings.

Suggested sequence only. Exact transfers, admissions, operating hours, group requirements and rates require written confirmation.
