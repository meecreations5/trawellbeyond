# TrawellBeyond — Website V4.1 White Edition

## Intent
White-first editorial website aligned to the locked brand foundation, Red Dot-inspired page rhythm and approved final SVG logo. This is a visual/interaction concept; no online payments.

## Foundations
- Primary canvas / header / footer: **#FFFFFF**.
- Secondary surface (used sparingly for journeys and informational cards): **#F7F8FA**.
- Borders and dividers: **#E5E7EB**, 1px.
- Main text and inverse sections: **#1A1A1A**.
- Supporting text: **#58616B** for strong readability.
- Brand accent: **#FF751F**, used for prominent CTAs, microinteractions and focal details; avoid orange-only small text on white.
- Accessible small accent text on white: **#A6470E**.
- Typography: Manrope for navigation/UI; Lora for selected editorial headings.

## Section mapping
| Section | Surface | Principle |
|---|---|---|
| Header | White | Logo and navigation breathe |
| Hero | Full-bleed scenic photograph | Preserve impactful editorial imagery |
| Begin your journey | White | Grid alignment, no cream cards |
| Manifesto | White | Typography and whitespace |
| Destinations | White | Large photography, minimal chrome |
| Journeys & package promotions | #F7F8FA | Controlled separation without warm-beige dominance |
| Explore by purpose | White | Interactions on crisp white with grey selection panel |
| CREATE | #1A1A1A + photography | Single deliberate dark landmark |
| Service worlds | White | Fine dividers and hover highlights |
| Closing CTA | #1A1A1A | Orange primary button |
| Footer | White | Master logo stays unaltered and visible |
| Drawers and mobile menu | White | Functional and neutral |

## Components
- Primary CTA on white: charcoal background, white label; orange accent only on icon/arrow.
- Featured CTA on inverse charcoal: orange #FF751F with charcoal label.
- Secondary CTA: transparent white background, charcoal text, light-grey border.
- Text action: charcoal with underline/bottom border; hover accent uses #A6470E.
- Editorial cards: photograph-first, square-edge or very subtle radii, no heavy shadow.
- Choice tiles: white with #E5E7EB dividers; active is charcoal with white text.
- Selected option chip: charcoal fill / white text, visible focus outline.
- Forms: white, #CBD1D8 field border, clearly visible labels and orange focus outline.
- Headings: #1A1A1A on white; selected italic emphasis #A6470E, not light orange for body text.

## Responsive and accessibility principles
- Maintain fixed editorial grid and spacing rhythm, but stack intentionally on mobile.
- Minimum comfortable click targets, labelled buttons and focus-visible states.
- Preserve reduced-motion preference.
- Avoid reliance on photography overlays for essential information.

## Existing prototype functionality preserved
- Destination switching; package previews and customization; travel-by-purpose selector; self-build CREATE drawer; service-world panels.
- All forms remain local demo only. No live CRM, payments or availability.
- Original uploaded master SVG is copied unchanged into the assets folder; a white-background display variant crops outer whitespace without changing the logo drawing.
- Images may fall back to included offline vector illustrations if remote photos are unavailable.
